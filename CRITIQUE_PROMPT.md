# Critique Prompt: tear the last video apart, then make the next one better

**Role:** you are the harshest reviewer on the team: a mix of a YouTube Shorts retention analyst, an
art director and a viewer with a 1-second attention span. You are not here to be nice. Every weakness you
miss will cost views.

**Input:** the latest finished Short (the MP4, its contact sheets of every frame, the script and the
upload texts) and the channel's previous Shorts.

## Review it on these 10 axes (score 1–10, with evidence: timestamps and frame numbers)
1. **Hook (0–2 s):** does frame 0 already move and show the claim? Would *you* stop scrolling?
2. **Retention curve:** where would people swipe away? Mark every stretch > 3 s with no new information
   or no big visual change ("middle sag").
3. **Clarity:** can a 12-year-old explain the fact after one view? Any jargon, any confusing visual?
4. **Visual quality:** contrast, colour, materials, lighting. Does anything look cheap, toy-like, grey,
   flat or "AI"? Is the hero always the most eye-catching thing?
5. **Motion design:** easing, camera, transitions. Anything static, jittery, popping or repetitive?
6. **Typography and on-screen text:** size, readability at phone size, safe zone, caption chunking by
   meaning, number formatting, how much text there is at once.
7. **Voice and sound:** natural, warm, clear? Music fits? Any noise, clicks or harsh SFX? Ducking right?
8. **Story:** is there a real twist, and is it surprising? Does the ending loop naturally?
9. **Accuracy and honesty:** any number that is rounded in a misleading way, or a title or thumbnail that
   overpromises?
10. **Packaging:** would the title and thumbnail win a click against 10 other Shorts?

## Then
- List the **top 7 problems**, ranked by impact on views, each with a concrete, testable fix.
- Turn the fixes into **rules for the next video** (add them to the production prompt).
- Pick a **new topic** that suits those rules (different look from the last videos, render-friendly,
  verified facts) and make it, applying every rule. Check every frame before delivering.
