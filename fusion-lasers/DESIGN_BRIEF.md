# Design brief: NIF laser fusion

- **Hook (frame 0):** the capsule implodes and bursts: diamond shards and glowing plasma fly toward the lens inside the gold can, with a big "192 LASER BEAMS" pill and the blast sound on frame 0.
- **WHY beat (≤ 25 %):** "Why do it? They want to build a star on Earth." A star flares up in the target chamber, then: fusion in the Sun (deuterium + tritium → helium + neutron + energy), "ZERO CO2", deuterium inside a drop of seawater.
- **Story:** hook → 192 beams converge in the 30-ft chamber → star on Earth → how the Sun shines → seawater fuel → gold can → pellet vs peppercorn → temperature bars beat the Sun's core → record shot 2.1 MJ in, 8.6 MJ out, 4× → twist: plants are still years away (the star shrinks and flickers) → "That's why, over and over," → frame 0.
- **Look:** Blender Cycles, black chamber, violet/white UV beams, gold hohlraum, icy blue fuel, white-hot core; sun set with an orange granulated surface and red/grey nucleons; teal seawater drop.
- **Scale:** capsule set in mm; chamber 1 unit = 10 cm (radius 5 m); hohlraum 10 mm, capsule 2 mm, peppercorn 5 mm.
- **Sound:** synth-pulse score (Dm Bb F C | Dm Bb C A). Every effect is tied to an event: zaps on the beams, a blast on the implosion, a snap and thump on the fusion, a drip on the drop, ticks on the counters, a boom on the burn, falling beeps on "years away".
- **Loop:** the implosion clock runs continuously across the seam (beams fire 1.2 s before the end, the blast folds into frame 0); the camera path is C1 across the seam; the voice reads "…That's why, over and over, / scientists blast a fuel pellet…".
