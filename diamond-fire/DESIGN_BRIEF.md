# Design brief (made under RULES.md)

- **Frame 0:** the diamond is already white-hot (yellow-white core, orange rim), sparks flying, the blue oxygen flame on it, "1,500°F / DIAMOND ON FIRE" on screen, a whoomp + crackle on frame 0. The hero fills ~90 % of the frame width.
- **A cut or big change every ≤ 2.5 s:** burning diamond → atom lattice (blue glowing bonds) → diamond next to charcoal → torch heats it (counter 70 → 1,500 °F) and it bursts into flame → hot crystal surface sheds CO2 molecules → gold ring on velvet → pencil drawing graphite → ring time-lapse with a year counter → the torch ignites the diamond again (loop).
- **No dark-on-dark:** every shot has a bright hero (glow, glowing bonds, gold, white paper).
- **Loop:** "So diamonds aren't forever. Look." → "This diamond is on fire…"; the diamond heats from cold to white-hot exactly into frame 0, the camera glides into the opening framing.
- **Look:** Cycles, AgX, sharp; black studio, warm fire colours vs. cool blue atoms; dispersion on the cold diamond.
