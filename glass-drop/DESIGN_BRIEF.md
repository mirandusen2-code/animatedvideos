# Design brief: "This Glass Drop Just Exploded"

- **Hook (frame 0):** the climax: the photoelastic rainbow head of the drop bursting into glitter on a deep navy glow, "3,000+ MPH / SHATTER SPEED" on screen, a whoomp + crackle on frame 0.
- **Look:** polariscope style: glass rendered with saturated rainbow stress fringes (emission ramp with dark gaps), dark navy spherical-gradient backdrops, warm orange for hot glass, ice blue for the cool skin.
- **Shots:** S0 burst (0–4.1 s) · S1 hot gob dripping into a beaker of cold water (4.1–7.9) · S2 cut-away: blue skin squeezing an orange core, inward arrows pulsing, "50 TONS" (7.9–13.0) · S3 hammer on the head, impact ring, "NOT A SCRATCH" (13.0–16.1) · S4 whole drop, red warning glow on the tail, cutters slide in (16.1–17.8) · S5 snip, crack front races down, camera dives into the frame-0 framing (17.8–19.0).
- **Loop:** "Snip it, and | this glass drop just exploded…"; the S5 camera blends into the S0 camera and the crack front reaches the head exactly at the seam.
- **Sound:** every effect is placed from the timeline's event list (snip, crackle run, head burst, splash, sizzle following the glass heat, squeeze thumps, hammer clang, warning beeps).
