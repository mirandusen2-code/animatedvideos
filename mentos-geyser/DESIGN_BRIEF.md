# Design brief: Mentos + Diet Coke

- **Hook (frame 0):** low angle on a 2-liter bottle on a sunny lawn, a foam geyser already blasting up past a basketball hoop, soda drops flying toward the lens, "MENTOS + DIET COKE" on screen, eruption roar on frame 0.
- **WHY beat (≈4.4 s):** "Why? This bottle is secretly holding three bottles of gas." In a teal studio, a see-through bottle full of glowing gas dots; three ghost bottles of gas pop out of it, "3 BOTTLES OF GAS INSIDE".
- **Story:** geyser past the hoop ("HOOP: 10 FT", "13 FT!") → WHY: three bottles of gas → the ghosts slam back in, the cap screws on, the gas rattles ("DYING TO GET OUT") → magnified soda: a bubble tries to start and collapses twice ("NOPE"), then grows in a rough spot → one candy up close → its surface: "CRATERS" → every crater spits bubbles "ALL AT ONCE" → the Mentos drop into the bottle and sink, bubble trails top to bottom, foam builds → the foam climbs the neck and shoots out: "UP!" → regular vs diet side by side, diet twice as high ("SWEETENER HELPS BUBBLES") → loop: "So stand back, and" a roll of Mentos falls into the bottle → frame 0.
- **Look:** Blender Cycles (12 SPP + OIDN, no motion blur); bright daylight backyard with a sky gradient, lawn, fence, hoop; creamy foam blobs and cola drops; teal studio with an amber see-through bottle and glowing gas; a cratered white candy landscape in brown soda with glassy bubbles.
- **Scale:** 1 unit = 1 inch; 2-liter bottle 12.6 in tall, radius 2.1 in; Mentos 0.8 in; rim at 120 in (10 ft); hero geyser peaks at 156 in (13 ft), regular soda 72 in.
- **Sound:** every effect is driven by the clocks in `timeline.py`: Mentos clattering into the neck and plopping in, a fizz, then the eruption roar following the jet strength, drops whooshing past the lens and soda raining on the grass; a thump on WHY, airy pops and chimes for each ghost bottle; thunks as they slam back in, the cap's ratchet, rattling gas and a creaking bottle; a bubble bloop and collapse (twice), a growing bloop in the rough spot; zoom swooshes and a shimmer on CRATERS; a rush of bubbles when every crater fires; clacks and plops for each Mentos, rising fizz; a rising pressure tone, then the foam jet's roar on UP!; two eruptions for regular vs diet; a swish on every cut.
- **Loop:** the eruption clock runs continuously across the seam; the loop shot moves from the bottle into the hook camera; voice "…So stand back, and / drop Mentos into Diet Coke…".
