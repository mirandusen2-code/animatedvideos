# What lives along the edge of a scallop shell?

- Some scallops have up to 200 eyes. The number is a reported upper bound. The three-dimensional animal illustrates a portion of the mantle and its eyes. [Palmer et al., Science (2017)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29191905/)
- A bay scallop carries rows of eyes along the mantle margins. Sections of intact eyes show the cornea, lens, two retinas and posterior mirror. The blue structures are eyes borne by living tissue. [Speiser et al., Integrative and Comparative Biology (2016)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5886045/)
- Cryogenic imaging revealed layered square guanine crystals forming a curved image-forming mirror. The reflection focuses light onto retinal tissue, an optical arrangement comparable to a reflecting telescope. [Palmer et al., Science](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29191905/) · [Guanine crystal formation, Nature Communications (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-35894-6)
- The cutaway is an explanatory optical section. Two layers of retinal cells are visible. It draws a single idealized axial ray bundle. Real optics vary with wavelength, object distance and species. The amber and turquoise rays distinguish incoming and returning light.
- No exact crystal dimensions, fixed eye count for an individual, or claims about what a scallop experiences are used.
