**Description**

Look closely at the blue dots lining a scallop’s shell. Each is an eye with a curved mirror inside. Researchers found that tiny square crystals tile its surface and reflect light back toward the retina. Follow a light ray through the eye, then see how many blue dots you can spot on the scallop.
