# Design brief: two grapes in a microwave make plasma

- **Hook (frame 0):** close-up inside a microwave: two touching grapes on the glass turntable burst open, an orange plasma ball with a purple fringe rising, skin, juice and sparks flying toward the lens, "2 GRAPES / IN A MICROWAVE" on screen, burst sound on frame 0.
- **WHY beat (≈4 s):** "Why? Physicists want to squeeze light into tiny spots, and grapes do it." Five waves converge on the grapes in a dark studio, "WHY?", "SQUEEZE LIGHT", then a close-up of the glowing contact with a reticle, "TINY SPOT".
- **Story:** plasma ball in the oven → WHY: squeezing light → one microwave with a 5-inch ruler → it enters a water block and shrinks to ½ inch, a grape appears next to the ruler ("= GRAPE SIZE") → cut-away grapes, rays bouncing into a star ("TRAPPED INSIDE") → two cut grapes touch, everything piles into one glowing hotspot with a pin beside it ("HOTSPOT", "PINHEAD") → zoom: salt ions, electrons ripped off, "BOOM, PLASMA" → back in the oven: two cyan water beads, no skin, same fireball → loop: grapes on the turntable start to arc → frame 0.
- **Look:** Blender Cycles (12 SPP + OIDN, no motion blur); grey enamel oven interior lit by the oven lamp; dark navy studio with glowing white wave lines and a glassy blue water block; green grape flesh with a purple skin ring; deep orange turbulent plasma with a thin purple fringe.
- **Scale:** 1 unit = 1 inch; grapes 0.8 × 1.04 in; microwave wavelength 4.8 in in air, 0.53 in in water; turntable radius 5.6 in.
- **Sound:** every effect driven by the clocks in `timeline.py`: oven hum and arcing crackle, the burst (pop, juice splat, plasma roar); a thump on WHY; wave tones and a shimmer; a ruler clink; a water bloop as the wave enters the block; a plop for the grape; pings on every bounce inside the grape; a rising sweep, sizzle and pin clink at the hotspot; zaps as each electron is ripped off and a boom on "boom"; bead hum, arcing and burst; a swish on every cut.
- **Loop:** the burst clock runs continuously across the seam; the loop shot drifts from the wide oven into the hook camera; voice "…So never do this at home: / put two grapes in a microwave…".
