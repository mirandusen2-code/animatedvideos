# Design brief: DART

- **Hook (frame 0):** the impact 0.3 s after contact: Dimorphos bursting, boulders and a dust cone flying toward the lens, "14,000 MILES PER HOUR" on screen.
- **Story:** impact → what was hit (system view, DART flies in) → the orbit clock ticks 11 h 55 → 11 h 22 → 6,000-mile tail → Hera arrives → twist: no crater, the whole moon changed shape → DART's own camera rushes in → cut to the impact (loop).
- **Look:** Blender Cycles, black starfield, single hard sun, grey regolith rubble piles; ICE/AMBER captions, red reticles, gold DART, silver Hera.
- **Scale:** 1 unit = 10 m; Didymos R 390 m, Dimorphos ~180 × 175 × 115 m, orbit 1.19 km.
- **Sound:** synth-pulse score (Am F C G | Am F G E), every effect tied to an on-screen event.
