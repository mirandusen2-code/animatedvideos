# A Honeybee Can Give Directions in the Dark

**Recommended title** · This Honeybee Is Giving Directions

**Three alternatives**
- Can You Read a Honeybee's Dance?
- Why This Bee Is Running at an Angle
- The Map Hidden in a Honeybee's Dance

**Description** · A returning honeybee uses the straight part of her waggle dance to share a route. Inside a dark hive, upward on the vertical comb stands for the sun’s direction outside. The angle tells the other bees which way to fly, and the time spent waggling indicates distance. Can you decode the final run?

Original 3D reconstruction and illustrated field map with an English synthetic male narrator. [NC State Extension](https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/honey-bee-dance-language) · [PLOS Biology](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.0020216)
