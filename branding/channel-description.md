# MindBlown 3D: channel texts

## Channel description (YouTube "About", max 1000 characters)

What if you jumped through the Earth? Why are sharks older than trees? How heavy is a teaspoon of a neutron star?

MindBlown 3D turns the most mind-blowing facts about science, space, nature and the human body into short 3D animations. Every video takes one surprising question and shows you the answer, so you actually *see* how it works.

🧠 Real facts, checked and explained simply
🌍 Space, Earth, animals, the human body & "what if" scenarios
🎬 Original 3D animations, made in Blender
📏 Both metric and US units (km/h & mph, °C & °F)

New Shorts regularly. Subscribe if you like having your mind blown. 🤯

Got a fact or "what if" question you want to see animated? Drop it in the comments!
