# Design brief

- Hook (frame 0): a chunk of the real Everest is already hitting the water: splash ring, "29,032 FT / MOUNT EVEREST" on screen, splash + thump sound, "You could drop…".
- Cut-away tank of the real Mariana Trench (DEM), water tinted darker with depth, sunbeams that die out at 3,000 ft.
- S1 depth gauge to 35,876 ft · S2 Everest on the floor with a bracket "1.3 MILES of water above the peak" · S3 glowing 3,000 ft line · S4 inch-scale macro: an 8-ton weight slams onto one square inch next to a ruler.
- Loop: the last shot shows Everest falling in from the sky again; the camera glides into the opening framing; the voice "It's so deep that…" flows into "you could drop Mount Everest…".
- Look: Cycles, AgX, sharp (no motion blur/grain), deep blue → black palette with amber accents for numbers.
