# Research: "Mount Everest Would Vanish in the Mariana Trench"

| Claim in the video | Value used | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Mount Everest height | 29,032 ft (8,848.86 m) | 2020 China–Nepal joint survey (National Geographic, Dec 2020) |
| Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep) depth | ~35,876 ft (~10,935 m), "almost 7 miles" (6.8 mi) | NOAA Ocean Exploration; Greenaway et al. 2021 (10,935 ± 6 m) |
| Water above Everest's peak if dropped in | 35,876 − 29,032 = 6,844 ft ≈ 1.3 mi, "over a mile" | derived |
| Sunlight runs out | sunlight zone ends ~660 ft; essentially no light below ~3,300 ft (1,000 m), "about 3,000 feet" | NOAA Ocean Service "How far does light travel in the ocean?" |
| Pressure at the bottom | ~1,071 bar ≈ 15,750 psi ≈ 7.9 short tons per square inch, "8 tons" | NOAA Ocean Exploration ("more than 8 tons per square inch") |

Terrain: real elevation data from the AWS Terrain Tiles (Mapzen terrarium, public), around Everest's summit
and around Challenger Deep. Heights are exaggerated 2.5× (the same for every object, so the comparison is fair).
The Everest chunk is simplified below its base (a tapered root) so it can be "dropped".
