## Description
The Mariana Trench is almost 7 miles deep (35,876 ft at Challenger Deep). Drop Mount Everest (29,032 ft) into it and its peak would still sit about 1.3 miles under the surface. Sunlight runs out after roughly 3,000 feet, and at the bottom the water presses down with about 8 tons on every square inch.

Sources: NOAA Ocean Exploration (Challenger Deep depth and pressure); NOAA Ocean Service (light in the ocean); 2020 China–Nepal survey of Everest. Terrain: real elevation data from the AWS Terrain Tiles.
