# Research Prompt: Find a Truly Surprising, Visual, *Verifiable* Fact for a 3D Short

**Role:** fact researcher + Shorts producer for the channel MindBlown 3D.

**Find 10 candidate facts** that make a viewer say "wait, WHAT?" within 2 seconds. Each must be:
1. **True and sourced**: a primary or authoritative source (peer-reviewed study, NASA/NOAA/USGS, museum,
   university). Reject anything that only appears on fact-listicle sites or is "commonly said".
2. **A comparison or paradox**, stated in one short sentence (e.g. "X is bigger than Y").
3. **Visual in 3D**: two or three big visual moments that can be built in Blender without characters
   (objects, landscapes, space, scale comparisons).
4. **Not about the same theme as our last videos** (Earth tunnel, neutron star / space).
5. **US + metric friendly**: numbers that can be said in both systems.

**For each candidate give:** the hook line, the mechanism ("why is it true?"), one escalation fact,
a twist/consequence for the end, sources with links, and a verdict: TRUE / TRUE-WITH-CAVEAT / MYTH.

**Then pick the winner** by: shock value × visual potential × certainty of the facts.
Write a 30–35 s narration (~85 words) with hook → mechanism → escalation → re-hook ("But here's the
crazy part") → twist → loop line, and a shot list with a new camera angle every ≤3 s.
