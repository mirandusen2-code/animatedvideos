## Description
A normal fluffy cloud weighs about 1.1 million pounds (500,000 kg), as heavy as nearly 100 elephants. ☁️🐘
So why doesn't it fall? Its water is split into trillions of tiny droplets that rising warm air holds up, and the air in the same space weighs over a thousand times more.

Source: USGS Water Science School (cloud weight).
