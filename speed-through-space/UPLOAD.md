## Description
Even sitting still, you're never at rest. Earth spins you around at up to 1,000 mph, races around the Sun at 67,000 mph, and the Sun carries the whole solar system around the Milky Way at roughly 500,000 mph. One trip around the galaxy takes about 230 million years — the last time we were on this side of it, Pangaea was one supercontinent and the first dinosaurs were just appearing.

Sources: NASA (Earth fact sheet; "Our Milky Way"; NASA Space Place); Langer et al. 2010 and Nesbitt 2011 on the earliest dinosaurs. Earth imagery: NASA Blue Marble Next Generation (public domain).
