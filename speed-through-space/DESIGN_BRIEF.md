# Design brief: "You're Moving at 500,000 MPH"

- **Format:** YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 17.9 s, seamless loop. English narration (7 % faster than the raw take, tighter pauses, to keep the pace up), captions synced to the measured word times.
- **Tone:** serious and awe-inspiring. Real NASA Earth imagery, a dark cinematic score, and big, clean numbers.
- **Hook (first second):** a bold "you" claim ("You're moving at half a million miles an hour.") with five things already happening on frame 0:
  - the camera rushes in toward Earth (a pattern interrupt);
  - stars streak past;
  - a "YOU" tag sits on the beacon over the USA;
  - a big speed counter is already climbing from 0 (about 440,000 after 0.7 s, 500,000 on the word);
  - a whoosh and a hit sound on frame 0.
  
  The counter reads "0 MPH" at the end ("…sitting perfectly still,"), so the loop restarts by firing it from 0 again.
- **Structure:** each line zooms out one scale (Earth's spin → orbit around the Sun → orbit around the galaxy), then turns the scale into time (one trip around = 230 million years) and pays it off with Pangaea and the first dinosaurs.
- **Loop:** "…So even when you're sitting perfectly still," → "you're moving at half a million miles an hour." The loop line was cut from the full sentence, so the intonation continues. The last shot glides back into the exact opening framing, and Earth's spin, the clouds and the streaking stars are phase-locked across the seam.

The speed is said only once (the hook). The middle lines name each motion without numbers, so the video does not repeat "miles an hour".

| Time | Line | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.5 s | You're moving at half a million miles an hour. | The camera rushes in on Earth; star streaks; "YOU" tag; the counter climbs from 0 to 500,000 MPH |
| 2.5–4.3 s | Earth spins you around, | Time-lapse spin; the beacon traces its latitude circle; "EARTH'S SPIN" |
| 4.3–6.1 s | it races around the Sun, | The Sun and Earth's orbit, Earth racing with a trail; "ORBIT AROUND THE SUN" |
| 6.1–8.1 s | and the Sun drags all of us around the galaxy. | Pull-back from the Sun's neighbourhood to the whole Milky Way; "YOU ARE HERE"; "THE MILKY WAY" |
| 8.1–11.7 s | One trip around takes 230 million years. | Top view of one full lap; counter 0 → 230 MILLION YEARS |
| 11.7–15.3 s | Last time we were here, the first dinosaurs were just appearing. | Pangaea globe; "230 MILLION YEARS AGO" / "PANGAEA" |
| 15.3–17.9 s | So even when you're sitting perfectly still, | No cut: Pangaea turns back into today's Earth as the camera pulls back. The USA turns into view, the beacon and star streaks fade in, and the camera rushes in to the exact frame-0 framing; "0 MPH" on "still" |
