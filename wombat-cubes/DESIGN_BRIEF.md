# Design brief: "Wombats Poop Cubes"

- **Format:** YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 21.5 s, seamless loop. English narration, captions synced to the measured word times.
- **Hook (0 s):** the claim "Wombats poop cubes." over a furry wombat already waddling; a cube pops out and lands with a thunk exactly on "CUBES".
- **Loop:** "…Scientists think that's why" → "wombats poop cubes." (one grammatical sentence across the seam). The last line was generated as the full sentence and cut after "why", so it keeps its rising, continuing intonation. The last shot glides back into the exact opening framing; the wombat's walk is phase-locked across the seam.
- **Look:** moonlit Australian grassland at dusk, a stylised wombat with real hair strands (sculpted SDF body, geometry-node fur), poop cubes with fibrous texture; x-ray scenes on a dark blue background.
