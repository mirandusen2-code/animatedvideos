## Description
*Turritopsis dohrnii*, the "immortal jellyfish", is smaller than your fingernail, but when it gets old, sick or injured it doesn't die: its body collapses into a blob that settles on the sea floor and turns back into a polyp, its baby stage. Its adult cells transform into new kinds of cells (transdifferentiation), and the polyp buds brand-new jellyfish. As far as we know, it can repeat this again and again.
In the wild, most still end up eaten. 🐟

Sources: Piraino et al., Biological Bulletin (1996); Pascual-Torner et al., PNAS (2022); AMNH, "The Immortal Jellyfish".
