# Design brief: "This Jellyfish Can Live Forever"

- **Format:** YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 19.2 s, seamless loop. English narration, captions synced to the measured word times.
- **Hook (0 s):** instant claim "This jellyfish can live forever." over a glowing jellyfish mid-stroke (motion from frame 0).
- **Loop:** "…So unless something eats it," → "this jellyfish can live forever." (one grammatical sentence across the seam; the video ends 0.05 s after the last word).
- **Look:** deep blue open water, light shafts, marine snow, moving caustics on the sand, underwater haze. The jellyfish is drawn like the real *Turritopsis dohrnii*: transparent bell, bright red four-lobed stomach and canals, a fringe of white tentacles.

| Time | Line | Picture |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2.5 s | This jellyfish can live forever. | Close-up, swim strokes; pill "THE IMMORTAL JELLYFISH" |
| 2.5–5.2 s | When it gets old or hurt, it doesn't die. | Turns pale, strokes slow, tentacles torn; pill with the species name; music cuts out after "die" |
| 5.2–9.1 s | It melts into a tiny blob, sinks to the sea floor, | Bell and tentacles collapse into a red blob that drops to the sand |
| 9.1–11 s | and turns back into a baby. | Stolons spread, a polyp grows and opens its crown; pill "BABY STAGE: POLYP" |
| 11–13.3 s | Its old cells become brand new ones, | Cell close-up: a wave turns dull old cells into glowing new ones |
| 13.3–15.5 s | and it grows up all over again. | A new jellyfish buds on the polyp and swims away on "again"; pill "NEW JELLYFISH" |
| 15.5–17.6 s | Again, and again, and again. | Rejuvenation pulse on each "again"; counter "LIFE #2 → #4" |
| 17.6–19.2 s | So unless something eats it, | A fish swims in and opens its mouth on "eats" |
