# Time the trip

A Moon seen from Earth gives way to an object astronauts left on its surface. Pull the viewer back to a telescope on Earth. One outbound and one returning pulse make the distance measurement visible. A ruler translates the long-term trend into an everyday US measurement, and the final question asks why the calculation divides the elapsed time by two.
