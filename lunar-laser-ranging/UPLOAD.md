**Description**

Apollo astronauts left panels of glass reflectors on the lunar surface. Today a telescope on Earth can send a pulse of light and time its return. Watch one round trip in about 2.5 seconds, then see what decades of measurements revealed about the Moon’s distance.

Original animated reconstruction with English synthetic narration. Lunar map courtesy of NASA’s Scientific Visualization Studio and the LROC/LOLA teams.
