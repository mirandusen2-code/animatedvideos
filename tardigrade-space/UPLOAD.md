## Description
In 2007, ESA sent dried-out tardigrades ("water bears") into orbit and left them exposed to open space for 10 days. Many survived, and some even had babies after the trip. 🐻‍❄️
Their trick: they dry themselves out, losing almost all their water, and come back to life when they get wet again.
In April 2019, the Israeli lander Beresheet crash-landed on the Moon carrying thousands of dried tardigrades. Whether they're still alive is unknown.

Sources: Jönsson et al., Current Biology (2008); ESA; Arch Mission Foundation.
