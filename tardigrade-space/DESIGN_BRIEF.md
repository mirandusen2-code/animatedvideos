# Design Brief: "This Tiny Animal Survived 10 Days in Open Space"
*Applies every rule from `../CRITIQUE.md` (max ~3.5 s per set, a visible change every 2–3 s, high contrast,
one saturated hero, labels ≥ 44 px, captions chunked by meaning, frame 0 moves).*

## Concept
**Macro meets cosmos.** A glowing, translucent water bear, lit like a jewel, alternates between two
worlds: the black of space (Earth's blue limb, hard sunlight) and a lush macro world (moss, water drops,
a steel millimeter ruler). The contrast between the two is the visual hook of the whole video.

## Hero
Tardigrade: chubby segmented body, 8 stubby legs with tiny dark claws, a round head with a small snout.
Material: amber/peach subsurface ("gummy bear" translucency) with a warm rim light: always the most
saturated thing in frame. Two states: **active** (legs out) and **tun** (legs pulled in, body curled and
shrunken, darker and drier).

## Palette
Space: black `#02040A`, Earth blue `#2E7BFF`, atmosphere cyan `#7FD8FF`, sun white.
Macro: moss green `#3E8E41` / deep `#14351A`, water `#BFE8FF`, steel `#B8BEC6`.
Hero: amber `#FF9A4A` → peach `#FFC59A`. Accent for numbers: amber `#FFB547`.

## Technical
Blender Cycles, 1080×1920, no motion blur, no shake, no grain. Background objects don't bounce coloured
light onto the hero. Everything that appears animates in or out.
