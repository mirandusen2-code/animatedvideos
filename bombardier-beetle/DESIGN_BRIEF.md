# Design Brief: "This Beetle Shoots Boiling Spray Out of Its Butt"
*Applies every rule from `../CRITIQUE.md` and the lessons from the mantis shrimp video: ~20 s seamless loop,
a visible change every 2–3 s, high contrast, one saturated hero colour, labels ≥ 44 px, captions chunked by
meaning, frame 0 moves, no early/fake flashes, no hard graphic lines, a clear two-step explanation.*

## Concept
**Tiny chemist, toad-proof.** A shiny black-and-orange bombardier beetle on a dusky forest floor. An ant
attacks and gets a boiling blast. An X-ray shows *how*: two chemicals flow into a chamber and explode. Twist:
a toad swallows it, the beetle keeps firing inside the stomach (X-ray), and the toad throws it back up alive.

## Hero
Bombardier beetle (*Pheropsophus*-style): orange head, pronotum and legs, glossy black wing covers with yellow
dots, a nozzle at the abdomen tip. Spray = soft, warm, pulsing steam cloud with a hot glow at the nozzle.

## Palette
Forest floor: near-black green `#020605` → moss `#0C1A17`, dark leaf litter. Hero: orange `#D96B0A`,
black `#020203`, yellow dots `#F29E0D`. Spray: warm white `#FFDCA0`. X-ray: ice blue `#40C0FF`,
chemical A blue `#0D4DFF`, chemical B green `#1AFF33`, chamber orange `#FF8C26`. Numbers: amber `#FFB547`.
