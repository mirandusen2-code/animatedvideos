# Research: how popcorn pops

- **Water inside:** a kernel holds roughly 14 % moisture, mostly in the starchy endosperm; the pericarp (hull) is nearly watertight.
- **Pressure:** heating turns the water to steam; the hull holds it until the pressure is about 9–10 atm (≈ 135 psi) (Hoseney et al. 1983; Virot & Ponomarenko 2015).
- **Popping temperature:** Virot & Ponomarenko measured a critical temperature of ~180 °C ≈ **356 °F**.
- **Expansion:** when the hull fails, the pressure drops and the gelatinised starch foams and sets; good popcorn expands ~40× in volume (expansion volume 30–45 cm³/g).
- **Jump / flip:** high-speed video shows a "leg" of foam pushes on the pan, launching the kernel a few cm and flipping it, like a gymnast (Virot & Ponomarenko 2015).
- **The pop:** the sound is not the hull breaking; it matches the release of steam (a Helmholtz-resonator-like pop) as the pressure drops (same paper).
