# Design brief: popcorn

- **Hook (frame 0):** the hero kernel mid-burst, foam flake growing, amber hull chips and white crumbs flying toward the lens; "356°F POPPING POINT" on screen.
- **Look:** dark warm studio, glowing amber backdrop, dark metal pan with heat glow; amber glossy kernels; matte bumpy cream foam.
- **Shots:** S0 hook burst → S1 wide pan with other kernels popping → S2 section: water drop in the hard shell → S3 steam dots and pressure counter 1→9× → S4 crack and 40× burst next to an unpopped kernel → S5 side view, leg kick and backflip → S6 close-up "POP!" with steam jets and sound rings, "NOT THE SHELL" → S7 loop: kernel heats, cracks and bursts into frame 0.
- **Sound:** warm chords; every event has its own sound from the same timeline as the picture.
