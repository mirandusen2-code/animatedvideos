# Design brief: "The Moon Is Running Away From Us"

- **Format:** YouTube Short, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 19.6 s, seamless loop. English narration (8 % faster than the raw take, tight pauses).
- **Hook (frame 0):** Earth's limb at the bottom and the Moon already flying away in a time-lapse. A live distance read-out ("238,855 MI — AND GROWING") climbs from frame 0, with a whoosh + hit.
- **Loop:** "…And it's still leaving. Right now," → "the Moon is running away from us." The last line was cut from the full sentence so the intonation continues. The last shot glides into the exact opening framing, and the Moon's distance is one continuous function of the loop clock, so position and speed match at the seam.
