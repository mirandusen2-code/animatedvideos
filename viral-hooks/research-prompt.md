# Research Prompt: What Makes Short Animated Videos Go Viral?

> Use this prompt with any AI that can search the web (or as a checklist for your own research).

---

**Role:** You are a YouTube growth strategist and scriptwriter who studies data, not vibes.

**Goal:** I'm starting a faceless YouTube channel with short 3D animated videos (made in Blender)
about surprising facts. Research what the most viral videos and creators in this space actually do,
and turn it into a reusable playbook I can follow for every video.

**Search the web for (prioritize 2024–2026 sources, creator interviews, retention data, and
breakdowns of specific viral videos over generic listicles):**

1. **Hooks (first 1–3 seconds):** the most-used and best-performing opening lines and visual openers
   on YouTube Shorts / TikTok / Reels. Collect real templates ("You've been lied to about…",
   "This is X. It can Y.", "Why does…?", etc.) and group them by type (curiosity gap, contrarian,
   shock stat, direct question, visual pattern-interrupt, "POV", list/countdown).
2. **Structure:** how top fact/explainer channels (e.g. Kurzgesagt-style explainers, Zack D. Films-style
   3D shorts, fact shorts) sequence a video: hook → setup → escalation → payoff → loop/CTA.
   Include typical timing in seconds.
3. **Retention tactics:** open loops, re-hooks every 3–5 s, "but here's the crazy part",
   pacing/cut frequency, on-screen captions, looping endings, sound design.
4. **Titles & thumbnails:** title formulas that drive clicks for fact videos, and thumbnail rules.
5. **Platform facts:** current Shorts length limits, aspect ratio, what the algorithm rewards
   (viewed-vs-swiped, average % viewed, rewatches).

**Output format:**
- A ranked list of **15+ hook templates** with a fill-in-the-blank version and one example each.
- A **video structure template** with second-by-second timing for a ~30–45 s Short.
- A **retention checklist** (max 10 bullets).
- **10 title formulas.**
- **10 ready-to-produce video ideas** (surprising, true, visual facts) each with hook + title.
- Cite every claim with a source link. Flag anything that's an opinion vs. backed by data.
- Then: **pick the single best idea and write a full script** (voiceover + on-screen text +
  shot list for a 3D Blender animation, 9:16 vertical, ~35 s) applying everything above.
