# Design Brief: "This Shrimp Can Punch Hard Enough to Break Glass"
*Applies every rule from `../CRITIQUE.md` (max ~3.5 s per set, a visible change every 2–3 s, high contrast,
one saturated hero, labels ≥ 44 px, captions chunked by meaning, frame 0 moves).*

## Concept
**Tiny boxer, huge physics.** A jewel-coloured peacock mantis shrimp in a dark-blue underwater world.
Real-time action (glass cracks, a shell explodes) switches to ultra slow motion for the twist: the
water tearing into cavitation bubbles that collapse in a flash of light.

## Hero
Peacock mantis shrimp: emerald segmented body with leopard dots, orange-red legs, bright orange antennal
scales, blue tail fan, two independently moving striped eyes on stalks, and two raptorial clubs (deep red
"hammers") that unfold forward in a fast strike.

## Palette
Water: abyss `#02080F` → teal `#08303D`. Hero: emerald `#1E7A3A`, orange `#E0471A`, club red `#8C0A1E`,
blue `#1A3FC8`. Flash: warm white `#FFF0D0`, shock ring ice `#9AD8FF`. Numbers: amber `#FFB547`.
