# The fish that carves a six-foot nest

The subject is the white-spotted pufferfish, *Torquigener albomaculosus*. The 2013 field paper used *Torquigener* sp.; subsequent work identified the species. The film is a procedural reconstruction of observations, with time compressed between construction stages.

| Spoken fact | Evidence and limits |
|---|---|
| The circles were studied off southern Japan. | Divers observed them off southern Amami Ōshima, in the Ryukyu Islands. [Kawase et al., 2013](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep02106) and [follow-up field study, 2017](https://doi.org/10.3390/fishes2030014). |
| A male about four inches long builds a nest over six feet across. | A synthesis describes a male of approximately 10 cm and a circle of approximately 2 m. These correspond to about 3.94 inches and 6.56 ft. The narration describes the size class of the observed nests. [Scientific Reports, 2018, introduction](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-30857-0). |
| The male swims inward, moving sand with his fins. Repeated passes produce radial valleys and ridges. | Divers saw males flap their fins against the bottom along straight paths. Later modeling describes repeated excavations hundreds or thousands of times. The animation compresses this sequence. [Original field study](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep02106) · [construction model](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-018-30857-0). |
| A female that spawns in the nest lays eggs in its central zone. | Field observations documented female visits, spawning in the center and subsequent male care. [Kawase et al.](https://doi.org/10.1038/srep02106). |
| The model carries small white spots and fine brown reticulations dorsally, a pale belly and yellow upper eye rims. | [Species description and field guide](https://www.fishbase.se/Fieldguide/FieldGuideSummary.php?c_code=392B&genusname=Torquigener&speciesname=albomaculosus). |

The film gives no universal nest diameter, construction time, egg count or claim that females choose whichever circle is most symmetrical. The final search is an invitation to the viewer. No stock wildlife footage, AI-generated wildlife imagery or field audio is represented as an original observation.
