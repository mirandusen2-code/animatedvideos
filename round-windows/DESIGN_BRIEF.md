# Design brief: why airplane windows are round

- **Hook (frame 0):** close-up of a white fuselage with a row of square windows; the hero window W0 tears out of the skin from its corner, skin shards and glass flying toward the lens, "SQUARE WINDOWS" on screen, rip sound on frame 0.
- **WHY beat (≈3 s):** "Why? Every flight, the cabin is pumped up like a balloon." A cabin cross-section swells and relaxes, "WHY?" then "8 LB ON EVERY SQUARE INCH".
- **Story:** window rips out → the whole jet breaks apart in the sky → WHY: the cabin is pressurized every flight → a stress map around a square window: the push lines flow around it and pile up red at the sharp corner → flight counter ticks, a fatigue crack grows from that corner → 1954: two Comets break apart over the sea ("THE FIRST JETLINER", "DE HAVILLAND COMET") → the water tank at Farnborough, pressure cycled, a counter to ~3,000 → it splits open at a window corner, bubbles rising → the window morphs from square to round, the red spot fades ("ROUND CORNERS", "NO WEAK SPOT") → "So next time you fly, remember:" → back into the tearing square window.
- **Look:** Blender Cycles (12 SPP + OIDN, no motion blur); glossy white airliner skin with a blue cheat line, dark glass; stress map in a blue→yellow→red ramp with white flow dashes; polished aluminium 1950s jetliner over a cloud-dappled sea; dim blue-green water tank.
- **Scale:** 1 unit = 1 ft; fuselage radius 5.5 ft, windows 1.24 × 1.56 ft at 3.1 ft pitch; jetliner 93 ft long, radius 5 ft.
- **Sound:** every effect driven by the same clocks as the picture (`timeline.py`): airflow roar, creaks and crack ticks, then the tear + decompression whoosh + the window frame flying by; rising hiss and skin creaks as the cabin swells; a hum and throb at the sharp corner; counter ticks and crackle as the crack grows; 1950s turbojet whine, then the break-up boom and debris rattle; underwater room tone with one pump thump per pressure cycle, the split snap + bubble gush; a shimmer as the square melts round and a ping on NO WEAK SPOT; a short air swish on every cut.
- **Loop:** the rip clock runs continuously across the seam; the final shot drifts back into the hook camera; voice "…So next time you fly, remember: / airplane windows used to be square…".
